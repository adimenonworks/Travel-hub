# Rove app spec: screens, flows and features

**Read this before changing a screen, flow or feature:** Home, Trips, a trip's tabs, events and the event page, Save a place, Collections, budgets, layovers, time zones, Recently deleted. It describes how the app works today. Planned changes are in `docs/changes/`; the reasons are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

**Moved word for word from `CLAUDE.md` on 14 Nov 2026,** when it was split (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`). Keep it current: when the app changes, update this file in the same pull request. If it disagrees with the code, Figma or `CLAUDE.md`, say so and fix whichever is wrong.

## Screens and flows

- **App structure (Figma Screens):** Home = now (greeting; two quick action Buttons that hug their labels, left-aligned: Plan a trip (Primary, calendar icon) then Save a place (Neutral, plus icon), never two Primary buttons; while a trip is on, a "Happening now" section: a small "Next up" heading (Heading 4), the next event as the deep-green Next Event card (as on Overview), then that trip's Hero card; then "Upcoming trips" (the soonest one with its people and heads-up row, the rest without (`TripHero list`); all without during a trip; "Upcoming trip" when there's one); Collections (country Tiles); Recently saved; with no trip or no saves, accent Cards with an illustration: "Plan your next trip", "Start saving places for your next trip"). Trips = library (Past with no trips shows the centred `memories` Card "No past trips yet"; Segment control Upcoming · Collections · Past, Upcoming by default, the picked segment remembered on the device; Upcoming and Past are Hero cards without people or a heads-up row (`TripHero list`: cover, name, dates, countdown), soonest / most recent first; Collections is country Tiles opening `/saved/:country`, ending with the Tile in Layout Add (the dashed "New place to go"; Tile is Layout Default / Add, no colour variants); segments change only by tapping, like every Segment control (owner, 11 Nov 2026); the list slides with `SlideSwap`); Upcoming ends with the same Add Tile, "Plan a new trip" (no button above the list)). You = profile (Profile card, Your travels Stat strip on the bright brand surface + map, Favourite places (3, most recently saved) with See more → `/profile/favourites` (a country Tile per country with favourites, A to Z) → `/profile/favourites/:country` (a section per city, or state/province when there's no city, A to Z; inside, a small titled List group per type: Food & drink, Stays, Activities), Settings list group whose rows open sheets (Home city, Currency, Maps app, Appearance) and Recently deleted (a page), Sign out (Neutral) and Delete account (Destructive; an "are you sure?" Sheet lists what happens, then Keep account / Delete side by side: you leave every trip and your messages are deleted, and a trip with no other traveller is deleted; your saved places are deleted except ones a trip uses, which go to someone on that trip; then profile, private settings and the sign-in, after Google confirms it's you; `store.deleteAccount`), then the app version with a small underlined Privacy policy link (`/privacy`, `features/profile/Privacy.tsx`: plain-language policy; update its date and text whenever Rove sends data somewhere new)). Figma also has Forward bookings, Notifications and a Support group (Help & feedback, Privacy, Rate Rove): not built until each has somewhere to go (booking emails, push, a privacy page, the App Store listing). A trip's Tab bar: Overview (Next stop: the next Event card in its Next state, deep green, no Itinerary link → Bookings: a List group, one row per type with a plain count → Recently viewed (the last documents, bookings and places opened on the trip, on this device, `src/lib/recent.ts`; compact rows with "Kind · time") → Countries you're visiting (was Good to know; meta "Emergency numbers and entry rules"): the trip's live list, a row per country from `tripCountries` (not your home country unless the whole trip is at home), "No plans yet" as the meta of a stop with no events (information only), each opening the Country sheet; it grows as events add countries, and deleting events never removes a stop while the trip is upcoming or on; no prompt to remove countries), Itinerary (the trip's days only, start to end: no days before or after, and end-day pickers (check-out, pick-up, drop-off, collect) offer only those days; Day cards: date number over the weekday, with a small month marker ("Dec", Label small, secondary) before the first day and where the month changes when the trip spans months or isn't in the current month; the selected one brand and larger; Event cards joined by Travel connectors with an estimated walk/drive time, the Now line; changing day slides only the plan; the selected Day card is wider (88 vs 64) and pushes the others aside smoothly; Travellers press and hold an event card for its Context menu, Edit / Delete (`HoldMenu` in Itinerary.tsx, about half a second, like iOS; it opens above the card when the card is low on screen; the tap that ends a hold never opens the event; followers don't get it). There is no row swipe since 11 Nov 2026 (owner: sideways swipes belong to changing day)), Ideas (To decide · Planned · Passed, grouped by city then "Links & notes", Idea rows sorted by votes with a "Most votes" tag, tap for the idea sheet; "Add to a day" schedules one: there is no dragging, by the owner's decision), Photos (album Cards), People (one List group, travellers first then followers; each row an avatar, the name ("You" under yours) and, for travellers, a role dropdown (List item Show Button: a small Ghost Button "Travelling ⌄") opening the Context menu: Travelling, Following (the current one ticked), then Remove from trip, or Leave trip on your own row; a role change confirms with a Toast with Undo; the last traveller can't make themselves Following. Followers see "Travelling" / "Following" as plain text and leave with a Leave trip button at the end. The last traveller leaving deletes the trip into Recently deleted, after a confirm that says so. Add people (travellers) opens a Sheet, "How will they join <trip>?", with two Option cards: Travelling ("On the trip. Plans with everyone and has their own bookings and budget.") and Following ("Not on the trip, like family at home. Sees the plan and chats, but never prices or bookings, and can't change anything."); Share link makes a 14-day link carrying that role (`invite.role`) and opens the share sheet). **Roles** (people and sharing item 1, 14 Nov 2026: everyone equal, no owner; `Role` = `'traveller' | 'follower'` in types.ts, read through `roleOf` in `src/data/roles.ts`; `useRole()` gives `role` and `isTraveller`): **Travelling** (plans everything: edit the trip's name, dates and cover, add people, change anyone's role or remove them, delete the trip (Edit trip → Delete trip, whose confirm names everyone else on it; it goes to Recently deleted for everyone and any traveller restores it), edit and delete any event, idea or album, hold menu; a link to join makes you one) and **Following** (sees everything and chats; adds, edits and votes on nothing; the rules enforce it, `isTraveller` in firestore.rules). Who started the trip is `createdBy`, a record only. Older trips store `owner`/`editor`/`member` (travellers) and `viewer` (a follower) and `ownerId`; the first traveller to open one migrates it (`useRoleMigration`). Joining: the share text is "Join us on <trip> in Rove"; the join page reads "<Name> wants you to join <trip>" with Join trip. Every trip tab has a small Primary Button in its Section header: Add event, Add idea, Add photos, Add people. Trip tabs change only by tapping (owner, 11 Nov 2026: no swiping between tabs). On Itinerary, swiping anywhere on the day's plan (event cards included) goes to the next or previous day, like paging on iPhone (`useSwipeTabs` in `src/app/useSwipeTabs.ts`). Segment controls (Ideas, Trips, everywhere) are tap only (owner, 11 Nov 2026). The view follows the finger (pointer events, `touch-action: pan-y`, so up and down still scroll), stretches at the first or last one, and on release changes if dragged past a quarter of the width or flicked (over 0.35px/ms), else springs back; the new view carries on from where it was let go (SlideSwap's keyframes read `--from`). A drag never counts as a tap. It never starts on a field, a map, a sheet, a Segment control, a strip that scrolls sideways or the left edge (swipe back). The trip header and Tab bar stay mounted; tabs are nested views that slide in the direction of travel (`SlideSwap`, 250ms, transform only; 150ms cross-fade with Reduce Motion) and each keeps its scroll position. Bookings rows open `/trip/:id/bookings/:group` (grouped by date, an "Add a flight"-style full-width Neutral button). An event opens its detail page (`ActivityPage.tsx`): flights, and transport with a from and to, use the Figma **Journey card** (`JourneyCard`, Layout Journey: a `.Leg` per leg (`JourneyLeg`): number and status tags, big times with the duration, terminals/gate/belt, "updated n min ago"; then Booking: reference, seats, bags; Files & links with Manage booking; Notes "added by"); other types have no card at the top: the Page header (Title small: the edit button top-aligned with the title) shows the category icon (Figma "Category icon", `CategoryIcon`: a 40px round tile in the type's colour; Page header "Show leading icon") beside the title, with the time as its subtitle (just the dates for anything with two ends), then, for stays, cars, cruises, parking and luggage, the Figma **Journey card**, Layout Span (`JourneyCard layout="span"`, was Span card: Check-in / Check-out, Pick-up / Drop-off, Embark / Disembark, Drop-off / Collect side by side with date and time, a car's places, and a footer: nights for stays, days for cars), then a **Route** section for journeys other than flights (and a car dropped off elsewhere): a From and a To row, each opening in the maps app, and Directions (`directionsUrl` in `geocode.ts`); then the Map card (map, place and address with a Favourite star Icon button in its Trailing slot that stars the Place in your Collections with its category, then the maps app and Uber, together) then sections, each only when it has something: **Booking** (company, flight number, booking or reservation reference with Copy, seats, bags), **Price & people** (price per person with its conversion, who's going; "People" when there's no price), **Notes** (added by). There is no separate "Add to favourites" button: favourites belong to the place, not the event. Before the chat, a collapsed **Changes** Accordion (`EventChanges.tsx`; shown once there's a change; meta "3 changes · last by Priya, 2h") lists the event's last 20 changes, newest first, one List item each ("Priya moved it to 14:00" / "Was 13:00 · 2h") with a Ghost **Restore** for travellers; Restore puts that part back and is recorded as a change too ("(restored)"). History covers title, date, time, place (only when the name shown changes, not when Rove finds the map position for the same typed place), price and who's in; it's written with the edit in one batch (`store.updateEvent`, `src/lib/eventHistory.ts`) to `trips/{id}/activities/{eventId}/history`, append-only. Saving an edit confirms with "Changes saved" and **Undo** (reverts the whole edit, also recorded); removing a file or link has Undo too. Every event page ends with its chat, Comments, Messages-style, in a grey section (`surface/secondary`; theirs are white bubbles) (`features/shared/Comments.tsx`, built from the Figma **Message** component, `Message` in design/components: Layout Theirs / Mine, Show meta, Show Avatar, Show edited; yours on the right in deep green, theirs on the left with the avatar at the top of the bubble, who and when above it; a run of messages from one person, each within 5 minutes of the last and with nobody else between, is one stack: one time line at the top and their avatar once, beside the most recent bubble; no delete button: press and hold your own bubble (or right-click) for the Figma "Context menu" (`ContextMenu`): Edit (the text goes back in the box with "Editing message · Cancel"; saved with `editedAt`, shown as "Edited") and Delete; while typing, the box stays in the grey section and the whole page scrolls so the section sits 16px above the keyboard (the keyboard's height comes from the `rove:keyboard` event in `setupNative()`, also `--keyboard-height`; a spacer the keyboard's height goes after the section, never inside it); Send works on the first tap (the button never takes focus from the box) and closes the keyboard; the send button is the Primary Icon button with the Send icon, emerald when there's something to send). New messages: while the app is open, someone else's message on an event in any of your trips shows a Large Toast with Open (`src/app/ChatAlerts.tsx`; not for the event you're on); until you open the event, its Event card carries a Neutral Tag "1 new message" / "n new messages" (Itinerary, Overview's Next stop, Bookings lists; `src/lib/reads.ts`: what you've read is synced across your devices in your private profile, `users/{uid}/private/reads`, one time per event so devices never clash). Lock-screen push alerts need the Apple Developer Program and a server: later. Rows with no data are hidden. No "I'm going" on events; prices per person only for bookings (see Budgets). Deleting an event from its page returns to its day. The trip header shows the title and dates only ("2 – 11 Nov · 10 days", `tripDatesLabel`), no chips. New trip is the full-screen flow (`features/trip/NewTrip.tsx`) on deep green (`feature/surface-secondary`, white titles; step bars then the close button; Primary footer): Where are you going? ("Add every stop, or just the first. You can add more later.": search, or tap a saved country, to add stops to a list under the field (flag, name, country, a small Ghost Icon button "Remove <place>"); the field clears after each pick; a place can't be added twice; tapping a selected saved country removes it; Continue once there's one stop; at most 30. Saved as `trip.stops` in order; `destination` is always the first stop and names the trip and finds its cover; older trips have no `stops` and are read as `[destination]` through `tripStops`) → Want to set a budget? (see Budgets) → When? (Exact dates: Calendar; Roughly: a month, saved as `dateMode: 'month'` covering the whole month, shown as "March 2027 · dates to confirm") → Who's coming? (Skip for now) → "Bring your saves?" (only your saves in the trip's countries, in stop order: places plus that country's links and notes; skipped when none match, and the step bars count one step less from the start, updating as stops are added or removed; each a List group (grey cards on the deep green screen); tap a country's city to open its types (Food & drink, Stays, Activities, each with a count, in its colour), tap a type to pick places; "All of <city>" and "All <type>" pick everything at that level; nothing comes along until picked; their Places become Ideas; Skip for now, saves can be brought in later from Ideas) → Celebration. There is no "Not sure yet": a trip without dates is just saved places. A trip is never created from a collection. **Places and Collections:** a Place (`SavedPlace`, the `places` collection, personal: `ownerId`) is the single source of truth; Ideas and Events point at it with `placeId` and never copy it. Collections are filed country → city → category, with links and notes that have no place (`type: 'link' | 'note'`) under the country in "Links & notes" (a country's page: the Figma **Map card** (`MapCard`, Location card and Collection header merged on 13 Nov 2026: a real map with every place pinned, tap for the full-screen map; "24 places · 3 cities"; then "6 of 24 visited · Saved by you and Sam" as meta text, with no progress bar; the Avatar stack in its Trailing slot), filter chips with counts: All, each type that has places (Stays, Food & drink, Activities), then Links and Notes when there are some, and Visited; a map card; a section per city (or state/province when there's no city; never a postcode: `cityFrom` in `src/lib/links.ts`, and `useFixPostcodeCities` repairs older saves from their map position) showing 3 with See all n, split into small titled type groups like Favourites); a Place's category is an **event category** from Food & drink, Stays or Activities (Restaurant, Café, Tasting, Winery, Hotel, Airbnb or holiday home, Museum, Shopping… — owner's decision, 11 Nov 2026), detected from the link or search (`detectCategory`) and changeable with the same type → category chip picker as events (`PlaceKindPicker` in `features/saves`, used by Save a place and the place sheet); A saved place's sheet (`PlaceSheet` in Collections.tsx): the place's name with its city and country; a key-info row (its Category icon and category, "Saved 3 Nov · from Google Maps · Visited ✓", a Ghost **Edit** that unfolds the category picker, and the Favourite star as a Ghost Icon button, filled when on); its photo and note; then **Location** (the Figma **Map card**, `MapCard` in design/components, shared with the event page and the collection page: the map, the place, Open in Maps in its Footer); a non-maps link as its own "Open on TikTok" row; footer Delete (yours) beside Add to <trip> ideas (not for Viewers of a shared collection). `PLACE_TYPES`, `placeKind()` (older saves' `shop` / `stay` read as Shopping / Hotel; nothing migrated), `placeTypeOf()` and `placeCategory()` (label, icon, type, tone) are in `src/lib/places.ts`. Links & notes: notes are emerald (Notes tone), links neutral. Save a place (`features/saves/SaveSheet.tsx`, opened with `useSave()` or a shared link) is a link field with Paste (Neutral, the only thing that reads the clipboard) and Continue (Primary, disabled until there's a link) side by side; no search, no mode chips. While the link is read only a skeleton shows. The preview has an editable Name (titles are cleaned by `cleanTitle` in `src/lib/links.ts`: no ratings, addresses, site names, hashtags or emoji; "Name on Instagram: …" keeps the name), **Saving to** (the country, Rove's pick, with Change, which opens "Choose a country": search or your saved countries; picking one goes straight back to the sheet, never saves; its footer is Done) with the country's **city Chips** right under it (Rove's pick, your saved cities there, + New city), and the **Category** picker (`PlaceKindPicker`: every category in three small titled groups, Food & drink, Stays, Activities; exactly one chosen, in its type's colour). Sections are 40px apart. It always files the place in Collections. With an upcoming trip and a place found, "Choose where to save this" (Heading 4) and a Segment control **Collections · Trip** sit under the preview (owner's sign-off, 11 Nov 2026: "Share to Rove"); it always opens on Collections (owner, 11 Nov 2026); tapping Trip picks the trip that covers the place's city. Followers don't see those trips there. Shared links title the sheet "Save to Rove" ("Shared from <site>"). Trip: trip Chips when there's more than one (the covering trip first), then three Option cards: **New event** (the detected category's tile; you pick the day from the trip's day Chips, today by default during the trip, and an optional time; Stays also pick a Check-out day; the event gets the Place's `placeId`, its name, place, zone and the link in Files & links; Primary "Add to <day>") or **Add to an event** (the trip's events by day, the likely match first and pre-selected: same Place, same name, same category, then the next one; the link is added to its `links`; Primary "Add to <event>"; the likely match shows first as "Suggested", then "All events" as one Figma **Accordion** per day, all collapsed, so a long trip stays short) and **Add to ideas** (Notes tone; an Idea pointing at the Place; Primary "Add to <trip> ideas"). Every Trip action also files the Place in Collections (reusing a duplicate). Toasts have Undo. **Finding the place** (`src/lib/links.ts`): Google Maps app share links (`maps.app.goo.gl`) open `maps.google.com/?q=<Name>, <address>` with no map position, and Google's page gives the address (`og:description`) and the type ("Lēoht · 4.4★ · Cafe" → Café, `kindFromTitle`); Apple Maps links carry `ll`/`coordinate`, `q`/`name` and `address`. `findPlace` uses the map position when there is one; else the name with its address; else **the address alone** (its city and country; small places often aren't on the open map but their street is); else, only with no address, the name alone. It never searches the name alone when there's an address (a Tasmanian café's name alone matched "Leoh", Ireland). Offline, `fromAddress` takes the country and city from the address text. It reads the link (`readLink` in `src/lib/links.ts`: in the iPhone app natively with `CapacitorHttp`, free, no server; elsewhere `store.readLink`, the readLink Cloud Function, NOT deployed because the owner chose not to use the Blaze plan; failing both, what the link itself shows plus a Photon search), merges duplicates, and confirms with a toast with Undo ("Saved to Japan · Tokyo"). The preview's "Saving to" row (above) opens "Choose a country"; with no country found, Save stays disabled until one is chosen. Saves are written without waiting for the server, so they queue offline. Ideas → "Add idea" offers Add from saved: Places in the trip's countries (every country it covers), grouped by city then category, then "Other saved places": your other saved countries, A to Z, each one collapsed Accordion with its places straight on it (for a country added after the trip was made); a place picked from another country becomes an Idea as usual, and ideas don't change the trip's countries. A Place is Visited ✓ once an event pointing at it has a past date (Visited filter Chip; unvisited sort first). The place sheet's buttons are "Open in Maps" (a Google or Apple Maps link, `isMapsLink`) or "Open link", and Add to favourites; never the site's name (Google puts the whole place title in og:site_name, so a maps save's source is "Google Maps"). Starring a Place, Idea or Event stars the Place (`starredBy`): ★ in Collections and Favourite places. Deleting a trip never deletes Places. **Shared collections** (11 Nov 2026, owner's spec): a country's collection can be shared by link (two roles shown as what they allow, **Can edit** and **Can view** (stored `member` / `viewer`); the person button on its Page header opens People, `CollectionPeople.tsx`: everyone with their role, "People you invite" Can view · Can edit, "Share invite link"; the owner taps someone to change role or remove them; others can Leave). A share is `collectionShares/{ownerId}_{countryCode}` (`memberIds`, `roles`); the owner's own places in that country are in it by being theirs, and a Member's place added to it carries `sharedIn` (the share id). **Can edit:** sees every place, adds places (Save a place offers "Your collection" / "<Name>'s collection" chips under Saving to), moves them (the place sheet's Edit has City chips + New city) and changes their category, stars them; only whoever saved a place deletes it. **Can view:** sees, stars and opens in Maps; nothing else (no Edit, no Add to ideas). A shared-with-you collection is its own Tile on Trips → Collections ("Portugal · Alex's", "Shared with you · Can edit") opening `/saved/:country/:shareId`; the Map card's meta says "Saved by you, Sam and Priya" (owner first) with an Avatar stack. Collection invites reuse `/join/:code` (`kind: 'collection'`, `role`), so the website's join page must be current. The rules enforce all of it (`shareRole`, `inShare` in firestore.rules). Not built yet: booking-email import (forwarded emails; also the source of booking references, seats, bags and Manage booking links).

## Features

- **Features to keep working:** **Budgets** (brought back by the owner on 10 Nov 2026; not a flagship feature): a trip may have `budget` (`Price`, **per person**). New trip asks "Want to set a budget?" right after "Where are you going?" (Option cards with descriptions: Yes → "Budget per person" amount + currency; No); Edit trip can set, change or clear it. Events you book ahead (everything but Food & drink, Free time and Notes: those are everyday spending) take an optional "Price per person" (`price`), shown on the event page with its conversion. Overview shows the Figma "Budget card" after Next stop when a budget is set: Left to spend (or Over budget in red), "of A$5,000 per person · A$3,187 planned", a bar, and the planned total per Bookings group (`useBudgetTotals` in `src/lib/currency.ts`, converting with ExchangeRate-API). No daily spending or expense logging. The old budgeting fields (`totalBudget`, `dailyBudget`, `dayBudgets`, `daySpent`) and expenses are still deleted once per trip by `budgetCleanup.ts`; the new `budget` field is separate. Ideas have a **Price** (`{ amount, currency }`, 0 = "Free"), shown on the Idea row when set; the Itinerary's Event cards show a booking's price per person (Event card "Show amount": Body small, secondary grey, top right level with the title, 16px from it; the title wraps; a stay shows it at check-in; not on everyday spending); older per-person `cost` values show as the price (`priceOf` in `src/lib/currency.ts`). The idea sheet adds a conversion to the home currency (Settings → Currency, private profile field `currency`) from ExchangeRate-API's open endpoint, cached 24h, credited in Settings ("Rates by Exchange Rate API"). Every event needs a location (except Free time, Rest and Notes): it adds the event's country to the trip's countries. **A trip's countries** (`tripCountries` in `src/lib/countries.ts`) are its stops' countries in the order picked, then every event's place (ideas don't count); everything that asks which countries a trip covers goes through it: Overview's Countries you're visiting, the entry-requirements heads-up, You → Your travels, Bring your saves, Ideas → Add from saved, the Save sheet's trip default (a trip covering the city, else the country) and Collections' trip row. Edit trip has no place picker or stops list (owner, 14 Nov 2026). **After the trip ends** only the countries its events were in count (stops never reached drop off, worked out each time from the end date; nothing deleted); a past trip with no events keeps its first stop. You → Your travels counts countries this way (plus places you added yourself); its cities still count each trip's first stop. Events (owner's spec "event types, categories, layovers and time zones", 10 Nov 2026; `src/lib/categories.ts`): adding by hand is two steps in the "Add to itinerary" Sheet. **Step 1:** 6 types as Option cards with what each covers, each in its colour (`EVENT_TYPES`): Getting around (Flight is its first, pre-selected category: Flights folded in on 11 Nov 2026), Stays, Food & drink, Activities, Free time, Notes & reminders. **Step 2** (titled "Add to itinerary"; and the edit sheet): a type row (the Category icon, the type and category, and a Ghost "Change type" that shows the 6 types as Chips under a "Type" label, so a wrong type is one tap to fix), then the type's categories nested in a grey inset panel titled "<Type> categories", a wrapping Chip row of its categories (`EVENT_KINDS`, 46, the most common pre-selected; the chosen one in the type's colour), one line under the chips saying what the chosen category covers (`hint`), the fields for its **behaviour**, then booking reference / price per person / notes / links, and the Primary "Add to <day>". The category sets the icon and the behaviour (`behaviourOf()`), never typed in; `type` and `behaviour` are also saved on the event. Behaviours: **journey** (Journey card; From and To each on their own line with Paste, "A place name, an address or a link from Maps" (a link is read with `readLink`; a typed name or address is looked up when saved, near the trip, so it opens in Maps); departs, arrives +0–2 days, flight number or operator, seat (not for taxis, transfers or private cars): Flight, Train, Bus or coach, Ferry, Airport transfer, Taxi or rideshare, Private car (your own car or a private driver)), **span** (shown twice, at its start and end: Stays (Hotel, Apartment, Airbnb or holiday home, Resort, Hostel, Camping, Staying with friends: Check-in / Check-out), Cruise (Embark / Disembark; port calls are activities), Car rental and Campervan (Pick-up / Drop-off; company, pick-up and drop-off places), Parking (your own car: Drop-off / Pick-up) and Luggage storage (Drop-off / Collect), both one place like a stay and able to end the same day (`sameDaySpan`: one card, "Drop-off 10:00 · Collect 15:00"), their cards show the place, not nights — `spanWords()`), **place** (Standard card; an optional Name and a Location, which takes an address, a place name or a maps link (Paste beside it; a link is read with `readLink` and fills the place, and the Name only if empty); day, time, "+ Add end time": all Food & drink and Activities, Metro or tram, Bike or scooter hire, Errand), **open** (quiet card, no place needed, times pre-filled with the day's first free gap of an hour or more between 09:00 and 22:00, `suggestFreeTime`: Free time, Rest), **note** (Note layout, white with a border; the text, its first line as the title, optional time: Note). Bookable categories have a booking reference (Restaurant: reservation reference); food & drink, free time and notes are everyday spending (`isEveryday`: no price). Older values fold in via `LEGACY` (stay → Hotel, activity → Sight, transport → Train, food → Restaurant, drinks → Bar, other → Sight). Every category has its own icon (reviewed 11 Nov 2026), Uicons Rounded built in Figma from the font outlines and generated into `icons.ts` from the same fonts (`@flaticon/flaticon-uicons` woff2, fontTools): Getting around: Plane (flight), Train, Coach (bus or coach), Ship, Taxi, Shuttle van (airport transfer), Train subway tunnel (metro or tram), Car side (private car), Bike, Car (car rental), Campervan, Car garage (parking), Luggage cart (luggage storage); Food & drink: Grape (winery); Stays: Hotel, Apartment, House chimney (Airbnb or holiday home), Swimmer (Resort), Backpack (Hostel), Camping, Ship side (Cruise), Users (Staying with friends); Activities: Camera (sight), Bank (museum), Flag (tour), Hiking, Umbrella beach (beach or nature), Music, Trophy, Party horn (festival), Ferris wheel, Spa, Graduation cap (class), Rocket (adventure), Disco ball, Shopping bag, Handshake (meet-up). Every category with its icon and colour is listed on /#/design ("Event categories"). No two categories in a type share an icon. Overview's Bookings rows are Flights, Stays, Getting around, Activities, Food & drink (`BOOKING_GROUPS`; no Documents row since 11 Nov 2026: files and links live on their events, so you find them by type; an event's row is `bookingGroupOf()`: flights keep their own row though they're in Getting around; `/bookings/car` now opens Getting around). **Layovers:** legs of one booking share a `journeyId` (editing a flight or other journey has "Add a connection" in its sheet, which adds the next leg from where it lands; it's not on the event page). The itinerary shows one Journey card for the whole journey on the first leg's day ("Melbourne → Lisbon · 1 stop", the layover "2h 35m in Dubai" under the title, total duration, "+1"); later legs don't get their own card. On the detail page each leg is a `.Leg` (`JourneyLeg`) in one Journey card with a **`.Stop`** (`JourneyStop`, was Layover block) between them: how long and where, a Warning tag "Tight connection" (under 60 min international, 45 domestic) or "Overnight in <city>" (crosses midnight or over 8h, with "Add a stay"); limits in `LAYOVER_LIMITS` (`events.ts`). Layovers are worked out from the legs (`layovers()`), never stored. Terminal changes aren't shown yet (Rove only knows terminals from live flight status). A journey landing on a later day puts "Arrives 12:40 from Melbourne" at the top of that day; one crossing the date line adds "You cross the date line on this journey". **Time zones** (`src/lib/timezone.ts`): times stay as typed (the place's local time) and each event stores its place's IANA zone (`tz`, a journey's arrival zone `toTz`, looked up from Open-Meteo by coordinates when saved; free time and notes take the zone of where you are that day, `zoneOfDay`). `toInstant()` gives the exact moment, so durations, "Next · in 1 hr", the now-line, Done states, `nextEvent` and flight polling are right whatever the phone's zone; nothing else on screen changes with it. Events saved before this have no zone and use the phone's. `eventCardProps()` in `events.ts` turns any event into its Event card. Rove doesn't track booked vs not booked. Countries you're visiting rows open the Country sheet (`CountryModal`): Emergency (numbers from `src/lib/emergency.ts`, only countries we're sure of) and Before you go: Entry requirements (the destination's official government site from the hand-made table in `src/lib/entry.ts`; never visa agencies; otherwise your own government's advice, Smartraveller by default), Local time ("Lisbon · 11 hrs behind Melbourne") and Currency ("Euro (€)"); button "Official entry rules". Times are 24-hour everywhere (`formatTime`, "08:30"); an event with a start and no end reads "17:30 onwards" (not notes or stays); Event cards put the time and the place on their own lines after small clock and pin icons; date ranges follow `formatRange` ("2 – 11 Nov", the year only when it isn't this year, day/month order from the phone's region). Tags (Figma "Tag", code `Tag`) are Neutral by default; Success / Warning / Danger only for a real status; there is no Tint tag (removed 13 Nov 2026) (the countdown is a Neutral tag, a white pill on covers).

## Who's in (people and sharing, item 3)

- Every event lists **who's in** (`people`, travellers only; read through `peopleOf()` in `src/data/eventPeople.ts`). Defaults: a plan added by hand or saved from a link, everyone; a flight or stay, just you; an idea added to a day, its voters plus you. You're always in what you add (your chip is locked).
- **Add / Edit event** has a **Who's in** row (a Chip with an Avatar per traveller) when the trip has more than one traveller. Picking another category picks its default again until you touch the row.
- **Only the people in an event edit it** (Edit, files and links, Restore in Changes). Anyone else travelling sees it read-only with **I'm in**. Followers see no buttons.
- **Itinerary cards:** who's in (Avatar stack, "2 of 4 going" or "Just you") shows only when it isn't everyone, or with a button: **I'm in** when you're not in it, **I'm out** while it's new to you (someone else put you in: Tag "New · added by Sam", cleared when you open it). Press and hold: Edit, I'm out (when others are in it) and Delete for people in it; I'm in for anyone else travelling.
- **Event page:** People section, "Who's in" row with the names and I'm in / I'm out.
- **Delete:** only you in it, straight to Recently deleted (confirm). Others in it, a sheet: **Just me** (I'm out, the default) or **Remove for everyone**. I'm in, I'm out and Delete each have Undo; changes to who's in show in Changes.
- **Older events** (`attendeeIds`) are migrated by the first traveller to open the trip (`src/features/trip/eventMigration.ts`): plans become everyone's, flights and stays keep who they named.

## Itinerary: yours, or one person's (people and sharing, item 5)

- **The Itinerary shows one person's events at a time:** yours by default (the events you're in). A **Person switcher** (Figma "Person switcher", `PersonSwitcher` in design/components: avatar Chips, Me then each traveller by first name, one selected, a radio group that scrolls sideways edge to edge) sits above the day strip when there's more than one choice. There's no combined view. The day's plan, its "3 plans" count, arrivals and the time zone follow the chosen person; the day strip stays as it is.
- **The choice is remembered per trip on this device** (`rove:itinerary-person:<tripId>`, `src/lib/prefs.ts`). Switching gives a selection haptic and slides the plan like days do (`SlideSwap`, in the switcher's order).
- **Someone else's itinerary:** on their events you're in too, a Tag "You're in"; on the ones you're not in, **I'm in** (travellers), no price and no unread-chat Tag. Their empty day reads "Nothing on Sam's day", with no Add event.
- **Events you're not in show only the title, date and time, place and who's in** (owner's rule, 14 Nov 2026), on the card and on the event page: no Booking, price, Notes, Files & links, Changes or Comments, and a line "Notes, files, comments and the price are for the people going. Tap I'm in to see them." Followers are never in an event, so they always see this view. **Until item 8 this is the screen only:** the event documents still carry everything, so the database doesn't enforce it yet.
- **Followers** have no Me; they start on whoever shared the link they joined with (`trip.addedBy`, written when joining and checked by the rules), else whoever started the trip, else the first traveller (`startingPerson` in `src/data/eventPeople.ts`).
- **Overview's Next stop, its Budget card and Home's Next up** count only the events you're in.

## Notification centre (people and sharing, item 4)

- **Where:** a Bell Icon button (Secondary, Show Badge) in Home's Page header, and the same count as a `.Badge` on Home in the Bottom navigation, so it's seen from any screen (owner, 14 Nov 2026: not in the Trip header). The Bell opens **Notifications** (`/notifications`, `src/features/notifications/`): newest first, grouped by trip.
- **Entries** (`trips/{id}/notices`, written by whoever made the change, for the people it concerns; never to themselves; text in `src/lib/notices.ts`):
  - **"Sam added you to Dinner at Ramiro · Sat 12 Dec"** (someone put you in an event, adding or editing it), with **I'm out** and **View** on a line under it while you're still in it;
  - "Sam took you out of Dinner at Ramiro"; "Jo is out of Fado night" (to the people still in it); "Priya moved Tram 28 to 14:00" (date or time changed; to the people in it); "Sam removed Fado night for everyone";
  - trip-wide: "Jo joined Portugal & Spain" (to everyone already on it), "Sam made you Following" (to that person), "Priya changed the dates to 12 – 20 Dec" (to everyone else).
  - **Unread chat**, one row per event you're in: "3 new messages on Fado night" (gone once you open the event). The slide-down chat alert while the app is open stays too.
- **Scope:** events you're in, plus trip-wide changes. Entries older than 60 days aren't shown. Undo and Restore are changes too, so they can send entries.
- **Read state:** opening the Notification centre clears the count (owner, 14 Nov 2026); what was new stays marked "New · 2m" while you're there. Kept with the chat read state (`users/{uid}/private/reads`, key `notices`), so it syncs across devices; the centre waits for it to load before marking anything seen.
- **Shaped for push:** an entry holds everything its line needs (kind, who, to whom, the event's title, day and time), so the same entry can later be sent to the lock screen (ROADMAP, Later).

## Recently deleted

- **Recently deleted** (owner, 11 Nov 2026; the owner called it an archive): deleting a trip (its creator only), an event or a saved place moves it to You → Settings → **Recently deleted** (`/profile/deleted`, `features/profile/RecentlyDeleted.tsx`) for 30 days (`KEEP_DELETED_MS`): `store.setDeleted(item, on)` sets `deletedAt` and `deletedBy`, and the data hooks hide it everywhere else (`useMyTrips`, `useTrip`, `useSub`, `useSubFor`, `usePlaces`, `useSharedPlaces`; `useMyTripsAll`, `usePlacesAll` and `useSubFor(ids, name, true)` include it). Delete toasts have Undo. The page lists Trips, Events (with their trip and day) and Places, newest first, each "Deleted 11 Nov, 14:05 by Sam · 29 days left" with Restore (travellers; places: whoever saved them); restoring puts it back where it was; an event whose trip is also deleted comes back as a Place in your Collections instead (not journeys or notes: restore the trip). After 30 days `usePurgeDeleted()` (in the Shell) deletes it for good from the first allowed person's device. Ideas and albums disappear at once and are deleted for good once their Undo toast has gone (about 6 seconds; straight away if the app is closed or hidden first: `deleteLater` in `src/lib/deleteLater.ts`). Comments delete straight away. Saving an edit to the trip, an idea or an album, and passing an idea or moving it back, have Undo too.
